# Triggered Deep Investigations

Create conditional references for deep-dive tracks. Each track must state its trigger, minimum evidence, stopping condition, and what remains unknown if it cannot be completed.

## Deep-Dive Tracks

1. **Full holder or Transfer replay** — triggered by supply discrepancies or historical concentration questions
2. **Complete pool and position history** — triggered by liquidity custody questions or lock/locker claims
3. **Launch-cohort accounting** — triggered by insider allocation or unfair launch allegations
4. **Fee-wallet and cross-chain proceeds reconciliation** — triggered by treasury or proceeds questions
5. **Proxy authority and critical bytecode reconstruction** — triggered by upgradeability or hidden control findings
6. **Reward-epoch accounting and backlog modeling** — triggered by reward sustainability or accounting questions
7. **External dependency and redemption analysis** — triggered by backing, redemption, or oracle questions
8. **Narrow operational attribution** — triggered by specific actor identification needs

## Bytecode Escalation Ladder

Escalate bytecode work gradually:
1. Runtime and selectors
2. Verified predecessors and compiler metadata
3. Storage and historical calls
4. Deeper reconstruction or simulation if needed

Decompiler output is not verified source. Claim executable equivalence only when compilation and byte comparison close meaningful differences.

## Routing

Route protocol-wide invariant work or substantial exploit campaigns to a separate audit workflow when available.
