# Report format, scoring and release

Use for formal reports and revisions intended for the Jeetstreet research program. Consume the currently released report schema/rubric from its owning repository; this reference is guidance, not a competing wire schema. The bundled EVM target-integrity manifest remains a different contract from a publication manifest. Do not coerce a legacy packet or Solana report into an incompatible schema to obtain a pass.

## Reader and machine envelope

Include stable report ID, immutable revision/digest, `reportKind: token_diligence`, exact chain/network/address, observation cutoff, creation/revision times, methodology/skill and adapter versions, evidence packet references, coverage limitations, editorial state, release classification and correction links. Record dependency generation when an actual gateway supplies it; unavailable is not a fabricated value.

Reader order: exact identity/time/stage; short conditional conclusion with strongest positive/material concern/principal unknown; Launch Fundamentals eligibility and coverage; examined scope; stable A–H findings; what would change the conclusion; methodology, accessible evidence and provenance/corrections. Price, turnover and executable-size estimates are dated observations, separate from fundamentals. Keep finding IDs stable and map them to the skill's A–H definitions rather than reusing those letters for unrelated categories.

Every material fact binds evidence, method, pin/time and bounded scope; label inference and unknown distinctly. Coverage describes requested versus captured ranges, markets, observed counts, reconciliation, decoder/source identity and gaps. Unknown source quantities remain null/not established. Do not derive full coverage from a page cap, sample, signature or equal counts.

## Launch Fundamentals

Use a **released, pinned item-level rubric** if available. PR 173 proposed A–H weights 20/15/5/10/15/10/5/20, 0–4 item ratings, separate assessment coverage and critical flags, and an 85% coverage gate plus resolved mandatory checks. These remain a proposal until an operational rubric fixes item weights, anchors, applicability and approval policy. Do not invent missing item weights or a calibrated scalar; use qualitative subassessments and “Not rated — insufficient evidence.”

With an applicable rubric: unknown is null, not zero/pass; legitimate N/A needs an approved reason; promised future functionality is not N/A merely because it is absent. Show exclusions and rated/applicable weight. The proposed conditional quality normalizes by rated applicable weight, so display the denominator and unknown sensitivity; it is not a confidence/profit probability. Do not multiply quality by coverage, double-count one defect opportunistically, or average away critical flags. Structural sellability and current execution depth are different evaluations.

Report publication can be eligible while a composite is withheld. Peer percentiles require a frozen comparable cohort, inclusion/censoring, sample size, methodology and tie policy; a few selected tokens or model reports are not a cohort.

## Corrections and verification

Preserve original evidence bytes, observation clocks and predecessor identity. A corrected report can replace prose in its specifically requested PR, with revision history explaining changed claims and uncertainty; a frozen/published artifact needs a successor, not silent overwrite. Do not make retrospective capture/approval claims. Model identity comes from authenticated runtime/operator metadata or is unknown; persona text is not a serving SKU. Disclose review exposure and human interventions separately from the original run's independence.

Recompute page-to-history identity **and body** equality, selected revision/header semantics, concentration groups/denominators, material amount/direction/fee arithmetic and any claimed retained executable comparison. A verifier checking saved booleans or mismatch counts is insufficient. Compilation rerun and retained compiled-byte comparison are different checks. Verifiers run offline without capture imports/credentials; do not execute unreviewed project scripts.

If the original workflow lacks a cumulative paid ledger, S3-first inventory or authenticated terminal receipt, state the gap; do not retroactively certify it. Disable or clearly quarantine unsafe historical capture/query entrypoints instead of promoting them as reusable operational recipes.

## Private evidence and publication approval

Current research-program default: raw captures, provider responses, row-level data, internal extracts, private manifests and operational identifiers remain private. Proposed public charts, summaries, methodology and supporting data require exact-digest artifact approval by the user. Existing Git exposure is not automatic permission to republish the corpus on a website or upload it to the new system.

Prepare a candidate release manifest binding report/version/digests, packet and dependency identities, validation, sanitized provenance, correction relationship, editorial state and explicit proposed artifact allowlist. User approval must reference the exact version; drafting, canonical selection, frontmatter or merging a spec is not release approval. The publisher gets approved objects, not broad archive credentials. Do not send Telegram/X messages without a separate request.

During migration, inspect the actual importer/scanner. The legacy scanner copies adjacent evidence and ignores richer editorial status; frontmatter alone neither makes a complete packet accessible nor enforces privacy. Root manifests/verifiers and gzip inputs need authorized navigation/downloads. Test status, MIME, actual bytes/hash and relative links; HTTP 200 SPA HTML is not successful evidence retrieval. Public citations use approved stable artifact URLs, never private locators or expiring signed URLs. Label internal versus public reproducibility honestly.

When a deployed release service exists, revalidate dependency generation/qualification synchronously immediately before publishing a current version. An asynchronous correction notification alone cannot prevent stale approval. On changed headers/revisions, quarantine or coverage, fail closed and require affected-claim review. Without the service, disclose the missing fence and do not invent a publication receipt. Later corrections create visible supersession/staleness; they do not backdate new evidence.
